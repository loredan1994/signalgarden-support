Signal Garden Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 21, 2026.

This policy explains how Signal Garden handles information in the iOS app.

Information We Do Not Collect Directly

Signal Garden does not require a developer account, email address, phone number, or personal profile to play. The app does not include separate developer-run analytics in the current build.

On-Device Game Data

The app stores gameplay progress, puzzle completion history, plant collection state, currencies, tool inventory, and purchase entitlement cache on your device so the game can continue between sessions.

Apple Services

  • Game Center may be used for leaderboards and achievements if you use those features.
  • StoreKit and the App Store process in-app purchases and purchase restoration.
  • CloudKit may be used to fetch game configuration and live content. Signal Garden does not use CloudKit for a developer-run player profile in the current build.
  • TestFlight may provide crash logs, diagnostics, and tester feedback to Apple and the developer when testers choose to share them.

Ads And Consent

Signal Garden may use Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform to display ads, manage consent where required, frequency-cap ads, and measure ad performance. Google may process device, ad interaction, approximate location, and identifier information according to Google's policies and the consent choices available on your device or in your region.

The app includes a purchase intended to remove automatic interrupting ads. Optional rewarded ads may remain available where implemented.

Support Requests

If you request support through GitHub Issues, the information you choose to post is processed by GitHub and may be public. Do not post private account details, payment card numbers, or sensitive personal information in support requests.

Children

Signal Garden is not intended to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child provided personal information through a support request, open a support issue requesting removal.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Signal Garden changes. The latest version will remain available on this page.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the public support page at https://loredan1994.github.io/signalgarden-support/.
