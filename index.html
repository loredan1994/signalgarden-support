Signal Garden Support

Signal Garden is a relaxing iOS puzzle game about routing energy and water through a greenhouse.

Request Support Privacy Policy

Getting Help

For bug reports, purchase issues, save progress concerns, or other support questions, open a GitHub issue and include:

  • Your iOS version and device model.
  • The Signal Garden app version and build number.
  • What happened, what you expected, and any steps that reproduce it.

Purchases

Signal Garden uses Apple StoreKit for in-app purchases. Use the in-app Restore Purchases button if an owned item is missing after reinstalling or changing devices.

Game Center

Leaderboards and achievements use Apple Game Center. Game Center sign-in and identity settings are managed by iOS.
